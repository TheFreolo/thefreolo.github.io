Research profile

I am a mathematician and computational scientist working at the interface of systems biology, quantitative pharmacology and model-informed drug development. I lead the Quantitative Systems Pharmacology (QSP) Group at COSBI, where we develop mechanistic models that connect biological mechanisms, drug exposure and heterogeneous patient response.

Group Leader — Quantitative Systems Pharmacology, COSBI · PhD in Mathematics, University of Trento

Research & PI profile Publications COSBI QSP Group

Translational QSP & PBPK

Mechanistic PK/PD and PBPK models for preclinical-to-clinical translation, target-site exposure, dose selection and model-informed drug development.

Virtual populations & digital twins

Methods for plausible-patient generation, population calibration, uncertainty, patient-specific mechanistic models and virtual clinical trials.

Disease modelling

Applications in tuberculosis and global health, Parkinson’s disease and neurodegeneration, lysosomal storage disorders and other rare or complex diseases.

AI + mechanistic modelling

Hybrid mechanistic/data-driven workflows, surrogate models, scientific knowledge extraction, model construction and reproducible computational research.

Current research direction

My current work focuses on making mechanistic models more useful for translational decisions: representing biological variability through virtual populations, moving toward patient-conditioned digital twins, integrating AI with QSP workflows, and strengthening model credibility for scientific and regulatory use.
